# Keep the Light

In **Wick**, you are the last lamplighter crossing an endless midnight field. You only choose where to walk; your tapers, embers, lanterns, and stranger tools keep their own relentless rhythm as the creatures of the dark close in.

The night begins with moths drawn to the glow, then thickens into flocks, swarms, armored crawlers, and towering horrors. Gather the light they leave behind to level up, add new tools, and strengthen the trinkets that shape your build.

Elite foes carry chests that can sharpen a tool, mend your flame, or transform a perfected weapon into something extraordinary. Every choice must carry you farther, because near morning even the Dark itself takes the field.

Survive ten minutes. Keep moving, keep gathering, and hold one warm circle against the whole night until dawn.
